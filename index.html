CHAPTER FOUR

If I Were There Today…

This is exactly how I would spend your birthday with you.

Morning

Breakfast in bed

Before you even woke up, I’d quietly get out of bed and make breakfast for you. I’d bring it to you with a small bouquet, wake you gently, kiss your forehead and whisper, “Happy birthday, Jaan.”

Later that morning

Your day, your choice

I’d let you take your time, shower, freshen up and get ready. Then we’d go somewhere you love, where you could look around, choose whatever you wanted, and enjoy the morning without thinking about anything else.

Back home

Slow down

You’d lie down and I’d give you a long head massage. No phone. No rush. Just my hands in your hair while you finally let yourself completely relax.

Lunch

Made by me, for you

I’d make lunch for you myself. Even if it wasn’t restaurant-perfect, I’d still stand there proudly like I had just cooked the greatest meal ever made. 😭❤️

Afternoon

Arcade time

We’d go out again, play arcade games, compete over stupid things, laugh too loudly, accuse each other of cheating, and turn tiny moments into memories we’d remember forever.

Evening

I’d help you get ready

I’d choose your clothes, help with your jewellery, sit beside you while you did your makeup, help with your hair, fix the little things, and step back every few minutes just to look at you and think, “How is this girl actually mine?”

Night

A long drive

We’d go for a long drive with music playing and your hand in mine whenever I could hold it. No rush. No destination pressure. Just us moving through the night.

Dinner

Candlelight & flowers

I’d take you somewhere beautiful and quiet for a candlelight dinner. Before we sat down, I’d give you the biggest bouquet of the day, kiss your forehead, and tell you how precious you are to me.

Still dinner

The quiet touches

I’d hold your hands across the table, play with your hair, keep touching your hand and shoulder, and steal more kisses than I probably should — because every little touch would be another way of saying, “I missed you.”

Home again

The softest ending

I’d give you another head massage, help all the tiredness disappear, cuddle you close, kiss you, and let you fall asleep on my chest with my arms around you and my hand in your hair.

Before sleep

One last whisper

And before I fell asleep too, I’d kiss your forehead one last time and whisper, “Happy birthday, my Jaan. I hope I get to give you this day for real someday.”

CHAPTER FIVE

Her, Through My Eyes

Eight little frames. Eight little ways I see you.

There are so many flowers in this picture, and somehow my eyes still go straight to you. I think that says everything. ❤️

This one has such a quiet kind of beauty. The look, the colours, the way you’re sitting there… you look like someone I could stare at for way too long and still not get enough.

You look so effortlessly elegant here that it almost annoys me. Like… how do you just sit there and casually look this beautiful? 😭❤️

This picture is just pure you — cute, playful, a little silly, and ridiculously adorable. The kind of photo that instantly makes me smile.

Something about you in soft colours like this always gets me. You look so calm, graceful and beautiful here that the whole picture feels warm.

This is one of those pictures where your cute side completely takes over. The thumbs up, that little expression… yeah, I’d probably tease you about this photo forever. 😭

This look is dangerous for me. The outfit, the hijab, the way you’re standing… every time you dress this elegantly, my heart genuinely forgets how to behave for a second.

You’re sitting there holding roses like they’re supposed to be the pretty part of the picture. Unfortunately for them, they never stood a chance next to you. 🌹❤️
